A stat's bonus is its value -10 and divided by 2 (18 becomes +4 bonus, 8 becomes -1)


### Strength
Melee damage: +bonus/attack
Carry capacity: 5kg + stat
### Dexterity
Melee accuracy: +5%/bonus
Movement: +bonus/2
Character evasion: +5%/bonus
Initiative: +bonus
### Constitution
Health: +(bonus\*classModifier)/level
### Wisdom
Ranged damage: +bonus/attack
Magical damage: +bonus/attack (class dependent)
Magical ability modifier: +bonus (class dependent)
Healing: +(bonus/2) per heal
### Intelligence
Ranged accuracy: +5%/bonus
Magical damage: +bonus/attack (class dependent)
Magical ability modifier: +bonus (class dependent)
Healing: +(bonus/2) per heal
